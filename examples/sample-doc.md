# The Little Store's Customer Handbook

## Refunds

We accept returns within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unused and in
their original packaging. Gift cards and digital downloads are final sale
and cannot be refunded once the code has been redeemed.

## Shipping

Orders ship within 2 business days. Some remote addresses may take an
extra week. If your package is marked delivered but never arrived, wait
3 days, then contact us with your order number.

## Loyalty program

Every dollar spent earns one point. Every one hundred points converts to
a five-dollar credit at checkout. Points expire after 24 months without
an order.

## Password resets

You can reset your password from the sign-in page. Reset links expire
after one hour. Contact support if you no longer have access to the
email on file.
